A marketplace that no company can switch off.

OpenFiat is an open protocol for exchanging stablecoins for local currency, peer to peer. Escrow settles on Solana; discovery, reputation and communication run on a network anyone can join. The exchange cannot suspend you, because there is no exchange — only participants.

Two layers, each doing what it does best

Solana performs what blockchains perform exceptionally well. OpenFiat performs what requires fast communication between people. The split keeps on-chain cost near zero without re-introducing an operator.

One trade, four moves

Protected from the second move onward — nobody in the middle can take the funds, and nobody has to trust the other side's goodwill.

01 · NETWORK

Reserve

You reserve part of a merchant's advertisement; every node records it.

02 · ON-CHAIN

Escrow locks

The seller's stablecoin moves into an escrow vault keyed by your reservation. Check it exists before you pay.

03 · FIAT RAIL

You pay

Bank transfer, mobile money or cash, directly to the merchant — the protocol never touches fiat.

04 · ON-CHAIN

Escrow releases

The merchant confirms and the program pays you. Disputes go to staked arbitrators.

The OPEN community presale

OPEN is the stake behind every role that keeps the marketplace honest — merchants, arbitrators, nodes, oracles. The presale offers the community allocation at a fixed rate before any public sale. No sale contract is deployed yet; connecting a wallet today only checks readiness.
