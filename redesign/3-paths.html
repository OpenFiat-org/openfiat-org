Stake OPEN for a role

Almost every role beyond buying does something that could hurt somebody else if done carelessly, so those roles put OPEN at risk before they get to do it. Staking is that deposit. It stays yours, and it can be cut if you misbehave.

01

What stake is, and what it is not

Stake is a deposit you keep ownership of, not a fee you pay to anyone. It is not reputation either, and it cannot be turned into reputation. What stake actually buys is skin in the game — a number the protocol can reduce if you break a rule, which is what makes strangers willing to rely on you.

02

Choose the role you are staking for

There are seven staked roles, and a stake account is keyed by (owner, role) — so one wallet can hold completely independent stakes under several roles at once. The role is fixed when you open the account.

The seven roles, and their on-chain values
Merchant                  = 0
Arbitrator                = 1
NodeOperator              = 2
NotificationProvider      = 3
OracleProvider            = 4
RiskIntelligenceProvider  = 5
SnapshotProvider          = 6
03

Get OPEN into your wallet

The devnet OPEN mint's mint authority is permanently unset — the full billion-token supply was minted once at genesis and nobody can ever create more. So there is no faucet: on devnet it has to be sent to you by someone already holding it.
