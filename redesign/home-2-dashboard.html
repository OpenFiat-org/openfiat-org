How a trade works

Four steps, and the money is protected from the second step onward.

01

Reserve

You reserve part of a merchant's advertisement; the node records it across the network.

02

Escrow locks

The seller's stablecoin moves into an on-chain escrow vault keyed by your reservation.

03

You pay fiat

Bank transfer, mobile money or cash — over your ordinary rail, directly to the merchant.

04

Escrow releases

The merchant confirms; the program pays you. A dispute goes to staked arbitrators, not a company.

What people use it for

  • KESA freelancer receives payment in USDC and converts it to Kenyan Shillings to pay rent.
  • USDCA business converts local currency into stablecoins to pay an overseas supplier.
  • PHPA traveler exchanges stablecoins for local currency while visiting another country.
  • NGNSomeone sends stablecoins internationally, and the recipient converts them into local cash.
